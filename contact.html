Relax, Celebrate & Reconnect with Nature

Welcome to Grace Ville, a beautiful private villa located in Udachiwadi, Saswad, just 33 km from Pune, near the proposed Purandar Airport.

Grace Ville is perfect for family vacations, weekend getaways, celebrations, get-togethers and relaxing day outings. The villa offers 3 air-conditioned bedrooms and 1 air-conditioned dormitory, with 5 washrooms and 3 kitchens, making it comfortable for families and groups.

We welcome both overnight guests and day visitors. Day guests can enjoy lunch, high tea or dinner, relax by the waterfall pool and spend quality time with family and friends in a beautiful natural setting.

  • Private Swimming Pool: With cascading waterfall feature
  • Entertainment: High-power JBL music system
  • Parking: Ample secure private parking on premises
  • Nature Trails: Surrounding mountains for walks & trekking
