| Welcome to Grace Ville

Your Private Hillside Sanctuary

Welcome to Grace Ville, a beautiful private villa located in Udachiwadi, Saswad, just 33 km from Pune, near the proposed Purandar Airport. Whether you are planning a peaceful weekend stay or a memorable day outing, Grace Ville is the perfect place to relax, celebrate, and enjoy nature.

The villa offers 3 air-conditioned bedrooms and 1 spacious air-conditioned dormitory, accompanied by 5 modern washrooms and 3 kitchens, making it exceptionally comfortable for families, celebrations, and large groups.

Enjoy our private swimming pool with a beautiful waterfall feature, surrounded by serene mountain foothills. For your celebrations, a high-power JBL music system is provided along with ample private secured parking.

We welcome both overnight guests and day visitors! Day guests can enjoy lunch, high tea, or dinner, unwind by the pool, and enjoy nature walks and trekking in the surrounding Purandar mountain trails.
