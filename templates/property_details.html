Grace Ville – Udachiwadi, Saswad (33 km from Pune)

Welcome to Grace Ville, a beautiful private villa located in Udachiwadi, Saswad, just 33 km from Pune, near the proposed Purandar Airport. Whether you are planning a peaceful weekend stay, an anniversary celebration, or a memorable day outing, Grace Ville is the perfect place to relax, celebrate, and enjoy nature.

Grace Ville is thoughtfully designed for family vacations, weekend getaways, celebrations, get-togethers, and relaxing day outings. The villa offers 3 air-conditioned bedrooms and 1 air-conditioned dormitory, with 5 washrooms and 3 kitchens, making it comfortable for families and large groups alike.

Enjoy a refreshing experience with our swimming pool featuring a cascading waterfall, peaceful surroundings, ample private parking, and a high-bass JBL music system for entertainment. The surrounding mountains also offer invigorating opportunities for nature walks and trekking.

Check-in: 12:00 PM | Check-out: 11:00 AM
Overnight guests have exclusive, private access to all 3 AC master suites and the group AC dormitory, 5 clean washrooms with hot water geysers, the private swimming pool with waterfall, rooftop sky terrace, and 3 kitchens.

We warmly welcome day visitors! Day guests can enjoy lunch, high tea, or dinner, relax by the waterfall pool, listen to music on the JBL sound system, and spend quality time with family and friends in a beautiful natural setting without needing an overnight stay.

The property features 3 separate kitchens equipped with gas stoves, refrigeration, appliances, and cookware. Guests can choose self-cooking, arrange custom local catering, or request barbecue and snacks by the poolside.
